04 · Questions

Straight answers.

Do I need technical skills?

No. We handle the setup. You tell us your most-asked questions, your prices, and your hours. After that, you keep using your Page inbox the way you already do — and you can jump into any conversation at any time.

What if the bot can’t answer?

It hands the conversation to your staff. The bot never guesses. When a question is too complex or sensitive, it stops and lets a real person take over — so no customer ever gets a made-up reply.

How fast can we go live?

It depends on your Page setup and how quickly you share your FAQs, prices, and hours with us. A simple chatbot can go live soon after we have your information. We give you a realistic timeline during your free audit, before you commit to anything.

What does it cost to run?

Your automation runs on Messenger’s free tier. You pay only the setup and monthly maintenance we agree on. If your message volume ever grows past a free tier’s limits, we discuss options with you first. No surprise charges.
